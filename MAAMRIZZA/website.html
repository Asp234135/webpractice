Your router is a tiny radio station

WiFi is not magic and it is not a beam. It is radio waves — the same family as FM radio — spreading out from your router in every direction, like ripples from a stone dropped in a pond. Your phone listens for those ripples and turns them back into messages and videos.

How the Signal Travels

Three things happen every time you tap a link

📱

1. Your phone sends

Your device turns 1s and 0s into a radio wave and pushes it out of its antenna.

🌊

2. The wave spreads

It moves outward in all directions, passing through walls and bouncing off metal.

📡

3. The router listens

The router catches the weak signal, rebuilds the message, and sends it to the internet.

2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz

Your router has two channels. Neither one is better — they trade.

2.4GHz

The long distance runner

  • ✔ Reaches about 45 Meters indoors
  • ✔ Goes through walls more easily
  • ✘ Slower speeds
  • ✘ Crowded — microwaves and neighbours use it too

5 GHz

The Sprinter

  • ✔ Much Faster
  • ✔ Less Crowded
  • ✘ Only reaches about 15 meters
  • ✘ Walls block it more

Where to put your router

The cheapest way to improve your WiFi: move the box

✔ Do this

  • Put it in the centre of the house
  • Place it high up, on a shelf
  • Keep it out in the open
  • Point the antennas in different directions

✘ Avoid this

  • Hiding it in a corner or a cabinet
  • Leaving it on the floor
  • Putting it behind a TV or metal shelf
  • Placing it next to a fish tank or microwave
