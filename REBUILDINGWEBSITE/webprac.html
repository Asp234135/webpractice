Your router is a tiny radio station
WiFi is not magic and it is not a beam. It is radio waves — the same family as FM radio — spreading out from your router in every direction, like ripples from a stone dropped in a pond. Your phone listens for those ripples and turns them back into messages and videos.

2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz

Your router has two channels. Neither one is better — they trade.

2.4GHz

The long distance runner

  • ✔ Reaches about 45 Meters indoors
  • ✔ Goes through walls more easily
  • ✘ Slower speeds
  • ✘ Crowded — microwaves and neighbours use it too
5GHz

The sprinter

  • ✔ Reaches about 45 Meters indoors
  • ✔ Goes through walls more easily
  • ✘ Slower speeds
  • ✘ Crowded — microwaves and neighbours use it too
